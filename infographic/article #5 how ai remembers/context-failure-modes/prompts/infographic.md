## Composition Notes
- Two distinct charts side by side, separated by a vertical chalk divider
- Chart A visually dominated by a horizontal segmented bar
- Chart B visually dominated by a vertical stepped timeline with warning icons
- Use colour contrast: Chart A leans yellow/orange (capacity warning), Chart B leans red/pink (contamination warning)
- Numerical stats in Chart A (30%+, 51K, 98.5%, 78%) should be rendered in larger, emphasised chalk — these are the anchors
- The shared footer "THE FIX" should be visually unified, spanning both halves, to make clear both failure modes share one solution
- Comfortable margins, no crowded text, keep labels readable
